Exp B — causal validation of ILP supernodes via constrained steering

Dataset: analogies / softmax / alpha_0.50 / node_0.02 · 100 pruned graphs · clusterer = ILP (Stage-2 MILP) · Exp B only (Exp D skipped). Output: edge_results.csv · logit_results.csv · summary.csv in this folder.

Headline: the ILP supernodes/edges are causally faithful. Negating a source supernode moves downstream supernodes in the sign predicted by the summary-graph edge 87.6% of the time (edge-weighted over 2,652 edge tests), and larger edge weights produce larger measured effects (median Spearman 0.50, positive in 92/100 graphs).

Configuration

method        = ilp        (theta=0, lambda_causal=1.0, max_layer_span=4, time_limit=30s)
steer factor  = -1         (paper negation: value = -1 · orig_activation, per CLT feature)
window        = [l, l+1]   (layers_below=0, layers_above=1 — corrected Fig. 9 direct-effect window)
freeze attn   = True       (constrained / direct-effect patching)
exp           = B only     (--skip-exp-d; per-node cohesion not run)
runtime       ≈ 92 min     (100 graphs, ~55 s/graph: ILP solve 5–15 s + steering passes)

ILP config matches what cluster(method="ilp") uses in production. The max_layer_span=4 cap keeps the MILP tractable on these graphs (28–57 middle nodes, mean 41 — above the 8–27 range that solves trivially); all solves finished within the 30 s limit.

Distribution highlights

  • Edge faithfulness: edge-weighted mean sign-consistency = 0.876; 57/100 graphs ≥ 0.90. Computed over 2,652 nonzero summary edges with a measurable source & target effect.
  • Monotonicity: Spearman(edge weight, |1 − activation ratio|) over the 1,459 positive feature→feature edges is positive in 92/100 graphs (≤0 in 8 — small/low-degree graphs where the rank correlation is noisy).
  • Logit effect: steering a single supernode shifts the target-token probability by ~0.17 on average (up to 0.40), confirming the supernodes carry task-relevant causal weight.

What Exp B measures

For each non-fixed supernode s: set every CLT feature in s to −1·orig_activation (negation), patch only over the direct-effect window [l, l+1] per source layer (attention & LayerNorm frozen), then read off the activation change of every other feature supernode and the Δ probability of every logit supernode and the target token. sign-consistency = fraction of summary edges whose downstream sign matches sign(edge_weight)·sign(Δa_source), weighted by |edge_weight|. The intra/inter/cosine_gap columns are NaN here because Exp D was skipped.
