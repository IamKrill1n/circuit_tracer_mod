\subsection{Attribution graph}
The attribution graph is a directed acyclic graph $G = (V, E, W)$ with
vertex set partitioned into
$V = V_{\text{emb}} \cup V_{\text{inter}} \cup V_{\text{error}} \cup V_{\text{logit}}$
(embedding, intermediate, error, and logit nodes) and signed weights
$W \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times N}$ on $N := |V|$ nodes, with $W_{ij}$ the
linear influence of source node $j$ on target node $i$~\citep{ameisen2024scaling}.
Embedding and error nodes are sources (no incoming edges), logit nodes
are sinks (no outgoing edges), and each intermediate node carries signal
between them, indexed by a transformer (layer, position) pair.
The reader also supplies a token weight $\tau \in \mathbb{R}^N$ with
$\sum_e \tau_e = 1$ marking the input tokens of interest, and a logit
weight $\ell \in \mathbb{R}^N$ marking the output positions of interest;
both enter the loss terms below.


\subsection{Summarization graph}
A \emph{summary} of $G$ is a triple $(V', E', \varphi)$ comprising
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,topsep=2pt,itemsep=1pt]
\item a pruned subgraph $V' \subseteq V$, $E' \subseteq E \cap (V' \times V')$;
\item a partition $\varphi : V' \to \pi(\varphi)$ where
$\pi(\varphi) := \{\varphi(v) : v \in V'\}$ is the set of nonempty
\emph{supernodes} and $K := |\pi(\varphi)|$ their count. Every
$v \in (V_{\text{emb}} \cup V_{\text{logit}}) \cap V'$ is a singleton
supernode; only the feature nodes
$V'_{\text{mid}} := V' \cap V_{\text{inter}}$ are free to be grouped.
\end{itemize}
The summary induces the \emph{summarization graph}
$G_{SN} = (\pi(\varphi),\, E^{SN},\, W^{SN})$. For each ordered pair
$(S, T)$ of distinct supernodes, the signed block sum
\begin{equation}
B_{ST} \;=\; \sum_{(u, v) \in E'} W_{uv}\,
\mathbf{1}[u \in S]\,\mathbf{1}[v \in T]
\label{eq:block-sum}
\end{equation}
aggregates the surviving edges from $T$ to $S$. We keep at most one
direction per unordered pair by retaining the dominant signed mass,
\begin{equation}
W^{SN}_{ST} \;=\;
\begin{cases}
B_{ST} & \text{if } |B_{ST}| > |B_{TS}|, \\
0      & \text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\qquad S \ne T,
\label{eq:wsn}
\end{equation}
with $E^{SN} = \{(S, T) : W^{SN}_{ST} \ne 0\}$. The tie case
$|B_{ST}| = |B_{TS}|$ emits no edge, so length-2 cycles are eliminated
by construction; longer cycles are addressed by the DAG cleanup step
(Section~\ref{sec:solution-cluster}).


\subsection{Desiderata}
A reader inspects $G_{SN}$ to recover the model's causal story. Three
quality conditions and two structural prerequisites determine when the
summary serves that goal:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.0em,style=nextline,topsep=2pt,itemsep=2pt]
\item[(D1) Coherence.] Members of each supernode share a mechanistic
role---similar influential downstream targets and similar relevant
upstream sources---so the supernode reads as a single concept.
\item[(D2) Conservation.] $G_{SN}$ preserves the causal mass flowing
from $V_{\text{emb}}$ to $V_{\text{logit}}$ in the original graph, so
the summary is not an artefact of what was discarded.
\item[(D3) Complexity.] $G_{SN}$ is small enough to read in one sitting.
\item[(F1) DAG integrity.] $G_{SN}$ is acyclic.
\item[(F2) Partition well-formedness.] $\varphi$ is a function on $V'$,
and embedding and logit nodes are singleton supernodes.
\end{description}


\subsection{Objective}
Each desideratum is operationalised as one loss term in $[0, 1]$; the
overall objective is their convex combination,
\begin{equation}
\min_{(V', E', \varphi)}\;
L \;=\;
\lambda_{\text{coh}}\, L_{\text{coh}}
\;+\;
\lambda_{\text{cons}}\, L_{\text{cons}}
\;+\;
\lambda_{\text{cplx}}\, L_{\text{cplx}},
\qquad
\lambda_{\text{coh}} + \lambda_{\text{cons}} + \lambda_{\text{cplx}} = 1,\;
\lambda_i \ge 0.
\label{eq:Lsum}
\end{equation}
We treat $\lambda_{\text{cplx}}$ as derived from the simplex constraint
and report the free pair $(\lambda_{\text{coh}}, \lambda_{\text{cons}})$
alongside results.


\subsection{Flow on the attribution graph}
\label{sec:flow}
The loss terms below need two per-node summaries of how causal mass moves
through $G$: how much each node \emph{receives} from the input tokens, and
how much each node \emph{sends} to the output logits. We obtain both from
the same flow construction.

Let $W_{uv}$ denote the signed edge from $u$ to $v$. Row- and
column-normalising the unsigned adjacency yields the forward and
backward transition matrices
\begin{equation}
P^{\text{out}}_{uv} \;=\; \frac{|W_{uv}|}{\sum_{w} |W_{uw}|},
\qquad
P^{\text{in}}_{uv} \;=\; \frac{|W_{uv}|}{\sum_{w} |W_{wv}|},
\label{eq:flow-matrices}
\end{equation}
defined to be zero on rows or columns with no incident edges, so embedding
nodes contribute no rows to $P^{\text{in}}$ and logit nodes no rows to
$P^{\text{out}}$.

\paragraph{Relevance.} Pushing the token weight $\tau$ forward from the
embeddings through $P^{\text{out}}$ and accumulating contributions at every
hop yields the per-node \emph{relevance}
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Rel} \;=\; \tau \,\bigl(I - P^{\text{out}}\bigr)^{-1}
\;=\; \sum_{t \ge 0} \tau \,(P^{\text{out}})^{t}.
\label{eq:rel}
\end{equation}
A topological ordering of the DAG makes $P^{\text{out}}$ strictly upper
triangular up to a permutation, so it is nilpotent and the Neumann series
terminates after at most $\mathrm{depth}(G)$ terms.

\paragraph{Influence.} Symmetrically, pushing the logit weight $\ell$
backward from the sinks through $P^{\text{in}}$ accumulates a per-node
\emph{influence}
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Inf} \;=\; \bigl(I - P^{\text{in}}\bigr)^{-1} \ell
\;=\; \sum_{t \ge 0} (P^{\text{in}})^{t}\, \ell,
\label{eq:inf}
\end{equation}
with the same nilpotence argument.

\paragraph{Edge-level analogues.} The pruning score in
Section~\ref{sec:solution-prune} reweights each surviving edge by the
flow values at its endpoints,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Inf}_{uv} \;=\; \mathrm{Inf}_v \, P^{\text{in}}_{uv}, \qquad
\mathrm{Rel}_{uv} \;=\; \mathrm{Rel}_u \, P^{\text{out}}_{uv},
\label{eq:inf-rel-edge}
\end{equation}
so that $\mathrm{Inf}_{uv}$ (resp.\ $\mathrm{Rel}_{uv}$) measures the
share of backward influence (resp.\ forward relevance) that traverses
edge $(u, v)$.

\subsection{Loss terms}

\paragraph{Coherence (D1).}
The \emph{role} of a feature $u \in V'_{\text{mid}}$ is its pair of edge
profiles, with each coordinate scaled by the importance of its endpoint:
\begin{align}
(v_u^{\text{out}})_k &= W_{uk}\,\sqrt{\mathrm{Inf}_k} \nonumber \\
(v_u^{\text{in}})_k  &= W_{ku}\,\sqrt{\mathrm{Rel}_k} \nonumber \\
r(u) &= \bigl[\,v_u^{\text{out}}\,;\, v_u^{\text{in}}\,\bigr] \in \mathbb{R}^{2N},
\label{eq:role}
\end{align}
where $\mathrm{Inf}_k$ and $\mathrm{Rel}_k$ are the per-node backward and
forward flow values defined in Section~\ref{sec:flow}. The square-root
weighting concentrates the profile on the edges that actually carry
causal mass. The coherence
loss is the mean rectified-cosine distance of each feature's role to its
supernode centroid $\bar r(S) = \frac{1}{|S|}\sum_{u \in S} r(u)$,
\begin{equation}
L_{\text{coh}}(\varphi)
\;=\;
\frac{1}{|V'_{\text{mid}}|}
\sum_{S \in \pi(\varphi)}\; \sum_{u \in S}
\bigl[\,1 - \cos_{+}\!\bigl(r(u),\, \bar r(S)\bigr)\,\bigr],
\label{eq:Lcoh}
\end{equation}
with $\cos_{+}(x, y) = \max\!\bigl(0,\, \cos(x, y)\bigr) \in [0, 1]$ the
rectified cosine. The cosine form is scale-invariant in $W$, and
rectifying keeps $L_{\text{coh}} \in [0, 1]$.

\paragraph{Conservation (D2).}
Conservation has two costs: \emph{pruning} (causal mass removed when $G$
is replaced by the subgraph $(V', E')$) and \emph{aggregation} (mass
cancelled or dropped when $(V', E')$ is contracted to $G_{SN}$).

\emph{Pruning loss.} We score the surviving subgraph by asking, in both
flow directions, what fraction of the flow visits non-error nodes. Each
feature dropped from $V'$ is first folded into its same-position
reconstruction-error node, so the error signal absorbs every causal
contribution the subgraph fails to capture. On this merged graph, let
\begin{equation}
\mu^{\text{in}}_{\text{err}}(v) = \sum_{\eta \in V_{\text{error}}}
P^{\text{in}}_{\eta v},
\qquad
\mu^{\text{out}}_{\text{err}}(v) = \sum_{\eta \in V_{\text{error}}}
P^{\text{out}}_{v \eta}
\label{eq:err-share}
\end{equation}
be the fractions of $v$'s normalised incoming and outgoing weight that
terminate at error nodes. Weighting each node by its flow in the matching
direction gives a backward and a forward completeness,
\begin{align}
\mathcal{C}_{\text{back}}(V', E') &\;=\;
\frac{\sum_v \bigl(1 - \mu^{\text{in}}_{\text{err}}(v)\bigr)\,
       \mathrm{OutInf}(v)}
     {\sum_v \mathrm{OutInf}(v)},
\label{eq:Cback} \\[2pt]
\mathcal{C}_{\text{fwd}}(V', E') &\;=\;
\frac{\sum_v \bigl(1 - \mu^{\text{out}}_{\text{err}}(v)\bigr)\,
       \mathrm{Rel}(v)}
     {\sum_v \mathrm{Rel}(v)},
\label{eq:Cfwd}
\end{align}
with $\mathrm{OutInf}(v) = \mathrm{Inf}(v) + \ell_v$. We combine them by
the geometric mean
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{C}(V', E') \;=\;
\sqrt{\mathcal{C}_{\text{back}}(V', E')\,\mathcal{C}_{\text{fwd}}(V', E')}
\;\in\; [0, 1].
\label{eq:Ccombined}
\end{equation}
The geometric mean penalises asymmetry: a high score requires error nodes
to be unimportant in \emph{both} directions, so a subgraph that preserves
downstream influence but loses upstream coverage (or vice versa) is
flagged. The pruning loss is $1 - \mathcal{C}(V', E')$.

\emph{Aggregation loss.} Contracting features into supernodes drops
within-block edges (self-loops in $G_{SN}$), cancels opposing signs
inside each block $B_{ST}$, and discards the weaker direction of every
unordered pair (Eq.~\eqref{eq:wsn}). The fraction of edge magnitude
not retained is
\begin{equation}
D_{\text{agg}}(\varphi)
\;=\;
1 \;-\;
\frac{\sum_{(S, T) \in E^{SN}} \lvert W^{SN}_{ST} \rvert}
     {\sum_{(u, v) \in E'} \lvert W_{uv} \rvert}
\;\in\;[0, 1],
\label{eq:Dagg}
\end{equation}
zero iff every pruned edge is preserved as a same-sign contribution to a
single directed inter-supernode block, and one iff every pruned edge is
absorbed.

\emph{Total.} The conservation loss averages the two contributions so it
also lives in $[0, 1]$:
\begin{equation}
L_{\text{cons}}(V', E', \varphi)
\;=\;
\tfrac{1}{2}\bigl[\,
  (1 - \mathcal{C}(V', E'))
  \;+\;
  D_{\text{agg}}(\varphi)
\,\bigr].
\label{eq:Lcons}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Complexity (D3).}
Reading complexity is the number of feature supernodes, normalised by
the unsummarised feature count:
\begin{equation}
L_{\text{cplx}}(V', E', \varphi)
\;=\;
\frac{|\pi(\varphi)| - |V_{\text{emb}}| - |V_{\text{logit}}|}
     {|V'_{\text{mid}}|}.
\label{eq:Lcplx}
\end{equation}
Embedding and logit singletons are fixed by~(F2), so the numerator is
exactly the number of feature supernodes. $L_{\text{cplx}} \in
[1/|V'_{\text{mid}}|,\, 1]$: the minimum at full collapse (every
mid-graph feature in one supernode), the maximum at all singletons.
