\section{Method Details}\label{app:method}

\subsection*{A.1 Flow quantities (Inf and Rel)}\label{app:flow}

Defining column- and row-normalized weight matrices
$W^{\text{in}}_{ij} = |W_{ij}|/\sum_k |W_{kj}|$ and
$W^{\text{out}}_{ij} = |W_{ij}|/\sum_k |W_{ik}|$, the Neumann series
$\sum_t (W^{\text{in}})^t$ converges on the DAG, giving
$\mathrm{Inf} = \ell^\top (I - (W^{\text{in}})^\top)^{-1}$ and
$\mathrm{Rel} = \tau^\top (I - W^{\text{out}})^{-1}$. Edge-level
analogues are $\mathrm{Inf}_{uv} = \mathrm{Inf}_v\,W^{\text{in}}_{uv}$
and $\mathrm{Rel}_{uv} = \mathrm{Rel}_u\,W^{\text{out}}_{uv}$. Rank
percentiles are $\widehat{s}_u = |\{v : s_v < s_u\}|/(N-1)$ and feed
the combined-evidence score of Eq.~\eqref{eq:CV-CE}.

\subsection*{A.2 Pooling choice for the combined evidence}\label{app:pooling}

The geometric combiner $(\widehat{\mathrm{Inf}})^\alpha
(\widehat{\mathrm{Rel}})^{1-\alpha}$ is the log-linear pool of
independent rank evidences~\cite{genest1986combining} and penalises
nodes (resp.\ edges) weak on either axis. Additive pooling allows one
strong axis to mask a weak one and is therefore unsuitable for the
``both axes strong'' criterion. The harmonic combiner is a stricter
analogue that further suppresses asymmetric scores.

\subsection*{A.3 Bidirectional affinity: matrix form and pairing}\label{app:affinity}

The matrix forms of Eqs.~\eqref{eq:Sout-Sin}--\eqref{eq:Sout-Sin-matrix}
are equivalent: $S^{\text{out}}_{ij}$ as a rectified cosine of
$v_i^{\text{out}}, v_j^{\text{out}}$ equals
$[\mathrm{ReLU}\,\mathrm{CosNorm}(W'\,\mathrm{diag}(\mathrm{Inf})\,W'^\top)]_{ij}$,
and similarly for $S^{\text{in}}$. The Inf/Rel pairing is forced by
geometry: targets of outgoing edges are downstream, so weighted by
downstream importance ($\mathrm{Inf}$); sources of incoming edges are
upstream, so weighted by upstream importance ($\mathrm{Rel}$). Any
other pairing weights an axis by an importance score irrelevant to
that axis.
