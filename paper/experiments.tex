\subsection{Datasets and setup}

Attribution graphs are computed on \textbf{Gemma-2-2B} via the
cross-layer transcoder pipeline of~\cite{ameisen2024scaling}, served
through Neuronpedia. The primary dataset is an \emph{analogies}
benchmark of $N = 55$ prompts of the form ``The saying goes: A is
to B as C is to,'' testing whether the model recovers the relation
between A and B and applies it to C. A multihop reasoning dataset is
held out for robustness checks.

\subsection{Planned experiments}

\item \textbf{Steering-coherence check.} Report
$\tilde{L}_{\text{coh}}$ on every row of the headline table. The
caveat of Section~\ref{sec:eval-graph} is that four of the seven
clusterers share the $r(u)$ affinity; this experiment verifies the
ranking on a method-neutral surrogate. Hypothesis: ASG variants
remain at or near the top under $\tilde{L}_{\text{coh}}$.

\item \textbf{Flow-faithfulness validation.}
$\mathrm{FaithCorr}_{\mathrm{Flow}}$, $\mathrm{Flow}\text{-}\mathrm{DAUC}$,
$\mathrm{Flow}\text{-}\mathrm{IAUC}$, and $\mathrm{Mono}$ per solver

\item \textbf{External steering.} For each supernode, scale the
activations of its member features by a common factor at inference
time and measure the resulting target-logit shift. Hypothesis: ASG
supernodes produce more predictable concept-aligned shifts than
baseline supernodes.

\item \textbf{$K$ sensitivity.} Plot scalar $L$ over the eigengap
candidate range of Section~\ref{sec:solution-cluster} to verify the
existence of an interior minimum.
