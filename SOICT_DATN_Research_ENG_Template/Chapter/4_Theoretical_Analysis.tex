It would be nice if we can prove that influence becomes less predictive of nodes importance as error builds up through the layers.
It is obvious that because influence mass is stuck at error nodes, the more layers we go, the less influence mass is propagated to the feature nodes. That is why even the original attribution graph doesn't have a perfect replacement score.
What is harder to prove is that influence order is wrong for feature nodes of earlier layers and embedding nodes.
